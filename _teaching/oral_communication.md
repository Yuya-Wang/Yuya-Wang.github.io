This course is designed to equip undergraduate economics students with the presentation tools and estimation techniques necessary to confidently articulate economic concepts, analyses, and insights to both expert and non-expert audiences. The focus is on undergraduate-level economics and econometric techniques that are commonly used in empirical economic papers. Students will choose an economic field subject to their potential graduate research interests, and present papers within that field. Basic Latex Beamer and Econ-style visual techniques will also be introduced. Offered Fall 2024.
